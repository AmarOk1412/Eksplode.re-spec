## End-to-End Scenarios

1. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8180/`. Confirm directory appears before any membership. Create Normal, Team and Chaser rooms using separate browser/CLI sessions; fill four participants with humans/bots. Start each, change inputs and finish one round: no other room's tick/state/settings/result changes. Repeat automated seeded play for 20 rounds per mode (SC-001).
2. Create a capacity-two password room. Wrong/missing passwords fail; correct admission fills it. Concurrent 100-client attempts at one vacant final seat admit exactly one. Nonowners cannot configure, remove, start or route actions into another room. Capacity below occupancy and Team/Chaser capacity below four fail. Delay a password worker beyond the five-second admission deadline and cancel another before completion: neither commits. Lose a committed reply and reconcile its original request ID: replay/query reports the same outcome, never another membership; a client timeout alone shows outcome unknown (SC-003).
3. Disconnect a participant without Leave. In a running match their slot continues under a bot. Resume within 60 seconds using the same browser session or CLI in-memory proof; current alive/eliminated/role/team state is retained. Drop Resumed, then retry its exact request ID/previous proof within the ten-second replay cutoff; drop AckResume/ResumeAcknowledged and retry acknowledgment with the stored replacement proof. Assert one rotation, one controller, unchanged original reservation deadline and rejection of the old proof after acknowledgment/cutoff. Fake-clock cases also cover forgery, simultaneous reclaim, removal, round end and last-human loss (SC-004).
4. Explicitly leave as owner; verify longest-present connected human becomes owner. Temporarily disconnect the owner; nobody else obtains owner powers until expiry. With two humans A/B, disconnect A and explicitly leave B: A's original reservation keeps the room recoverable until its deadline; B's proof is revoked immediately. With no remaining reservations the final explicit departure closes immediately. Run 100 create/play/close cycles and verify empty directory/resources return to baseline (SC-006).
5. Create identically named rooms on alpha and beta. Verify both are distinguishable in alpha's known directory and join beta: the browser goes to beta's origin; terminal joins beta's game address. Beta alone decides password/capacity. No secret appears in URL. Stop beta: alpha's local/healthy cached directory remains usable within five seconds, independently of failure detection. Measure detection against five seconds until poll due + worker queue wait + two seconds for the entire attempt: at most seven seconds in this guide and 13 seconds with 16 configured sources/four fair workers. Inject worst-case queueing/timeout in fake-clock tests; stale summaries disappear 15 seconds after the last successful refresh (SC-005).
6. Use keyboard only to list/create/protect/join/configure/remove/leave/rejoin; repeat with selected controller in the browser, then terminal keys. Check focus restoration, native selects, on-screen keyboard and no gameplay while typing. At 360x640 and 844x390 verify touch controls, multi-touch, conditional Punch, orientation reset and persistent results. Physical-device checks include Firefox for Android and iOS Safari when available (SC-007).
7. Exercise every event category in the operations contract. Parse stdout as JSON Lines; correlate server/epoch/room/member/revision and match ticks. Seed canary passwords/proofs in tests and ensure no sink contains them. Ordinary three-room load must record every significant event with zero drops. Separately saturate the queue or inject a recoverable writer failure: rooms continue, every undelivered record is counted, and output recovery emits the exact accumulated loss count. Permanent broken pipe/shutdown cannot hang the process (SC-008).
8. Measure at least 100 healthy local directory/join attempts and input feedback under three-room load: p95 directory/join <=2 seconds and feedback <=200 ms. Test maximum rooms/connections, flood, oversized JSON/directory pages, unsupported versions, incompatible peers, discovery timeout and password-worker queue saturation without cross-room changes (SC-002).
